A [World of Warships](//worldofwarships.com) encyclopedia Discord bot with ship build sharing and ship encyclopedia.

mackbot is designed to help new and existing players to be familiar with their warships, upgrades, or skills, while not in-game or while in a heated debate in their Discord server.

Invite mackbot to your discord server through this <a href="https://discord.com/api/oauth2/authorize?client_id=667866391231332353&permissions=2147796992&scope=bot%20applications.commands">invite link</a>.
